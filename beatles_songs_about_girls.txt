Tracking the Beatles' love songs over their career
by Hroghgar

-----

In this analysis, I'm using "Love Song" to refer to a song that is primarily about a romantic connection or loss/lack of one. Songs about pining for someone, losing someone, being sad about someone treating you badly, telling another guy that someone's in love with him, recounting a romantic encounter, etc, all count. Songs whose subject matter are girls but which are non-romantic in content do not count.

I've added notes about songs which are on the line one way or another.

I've omitted cover songs in this analysis.

Currently this list doesn't include singles that didn't appear on studio albums, of which there were many.

The track listings and albums I'm using come from the "standardized albums" list:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles_albums_discography#Standardised_studio_albums

Lyrics reference:
https://www.beatleslyrics.org/index_files/Page344.htm

-----

Please Please Me
    14 songs (6 covers)
    8/8 original songs are about love/romance/girl

With the Beatles
    14 songs (6 covers)
    8/8 original songs are about love/romance/girl

A Hard Day's Night
    13 songs (0 covers)
    13/13 original songs are about love/romance/girl

Beatles for Sale
    14 songs (6 covers)
    8/8 original songs are about love/romance/girl

Help!
    14 songs (2 covers)
    11/12 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE NOT about love/romance/girl:
        - "Help!"

Rubber Soul
    14 songs (0 covers)
    11/14 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE NOT about love/romance/girl:
        - "Nowhere Man"
        - "Think for Yourself"
        - "The Word" (This song is about love, but not about a girl or romance specifically. I don't call it a love song, I call it a song about the concept of love, which is different. It can easily be interpreted to be about fraternal love or love for the world in a greater sense.)
    Notes:
        - "Norwegian Wood" is arguably a not a love song, if you don't know what it's about. But it is in fact about a prostitute and its original title was "Knowing She Would"

Revolver
    14 songs (0 covers)
    5/14 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl:
        - "Love You To"
        - "Here, There and Everywhere"
        - "Good Day Sunshine"
        - "For No One"
        - "Got to Get You Into My Life"
    Maybes:
        - "She Said She Said" depicts a conversation with a girl but cannot be said to be romantic
        - "I Want To Tell You" certainly invites interpretation as a love song but cannot be said to definitively be one

Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band
    13 songs (0 covers)
    2/13 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl:
        - "When I'm Sixty-Four"
        - "Lovely Rita"
    Notes:
        - Paul is holding it down here, writing the only love songs on this album
        - "With a Little Help from My Friends" has the line "I need somebody to love" but it's not a love song
        - "Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds" is about Lucy and her eyes but again I can't say it's a love song in any form, it simply takes Lucy as subject matter
        - "Within You Without You" is very psychedelic and arguably is a psychedelic love song, if only because of "with our love / we could save the world / if they only knew"... but it's hard to make that argument given the rest of it, in my opinion

Magical Mystery Tour
    11 songs (0 covers)
    0/11 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Notes:
        - "All You Need Is Love" is the only song with the word "love" in it at all, and it's not a love song
        - This album has one instrumental song, "Flying"

The Beatles (The White Album)
    30 songs (0 covers)
    12/30 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl:
        - "Back in the U.S.S.R." (ultimately a love song in the sense I describe above)
        - "Wild Honey Pie" (I guess)
        - "Martha My Dear"
        - "Don't Pass Me By"
        - "Why Don’t We Do it in the Road?" (I guess)
        - "I Will"
        - "Julia"
        - "Yer Blues" (I guess)
        - "Sexy Sadie" (up for debate)
        - "Helter Skelter"
        - "Long, Long, Long"
        - "Honey Pie"

    Notes:
        - "Ob-La-Di, Ob-La-Da"... this one I don't know how to classify. It is about a romance between two people, but a story-telling. I'm inclined not to include it, but I don't know.
        - Similarly, "Rocky Raccoon" is a story-telling of a romantic disaster. Not sure how to count it. It's categorically different from something like "Love Me Do".

Yellow Submarine
    13 songs (0 covers)
    1/13 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl:
        - "It's All Too Much" (psychedelia, I'm going to say it's a love song but definitely up for debate)
    Notes:
        - "All Together Now" is kind of on the line
        - This album has 7 instrumentals and includes "All You Need Is Love" which was already counted above on Magical Mystery Tour

Abbey Road
    16 songs (0 covers)
    4/16 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl:
        - "Something"
        - "Oh! Darling"
        - "Octopus's Garden" (up for debate but the obvious interpretation is of it as a sort of love song)
        - "I Want You (She's So Heavy)"
    Notes:
        - other than saying "little darling" over and over, "Here Comes the Sun" doesn't seem to be a love song
        - "Polythene Pam" is kind of on the line maybe but I am not counting it as a love song
        - "She Came in Through the Bathroom Window" is also interpretable as a love song, but I say no

Let It Be
    12 songs (0 covers)
    3/12 original songs are about love/romance/girl
    Songs that ARE about love/romance/girl: 
        - "I've Got a Feeling" (up for debate)
        - "One After 909"
        - "For You Blue"
    Notes:
        - I interpret "Two of Us" as being about Lennon and McCartney, not a love song in the traditional sense
        - "I Dig a Pony" is hard to judge... the repeated line "all I want is you" makes a compelling case for love song, but the rest of it is nonsense, so
        - "The Long and Winding Road" is, I suppose, up for some debate too but I say no (pretty conclusively)
